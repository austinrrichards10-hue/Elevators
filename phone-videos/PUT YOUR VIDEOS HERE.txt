PHONE VIDEOS (the TikTok app)
=============================

Put video files in this folder and they show up in the TikTok app on Miku's phone in Tokyo
(press P in the city and open TikTok).

Name them with numbers, in order, with no gaps:

    1.mp4
    2.mp4
    3.webm
    4.mp4
    ...

The formats are .mp4 (best), .webm, .mov and .m4v. Each number can be any of those.
(video1.mp4, video2.mp4, ... also works.)

Why numbers? When you open index.html straight from your computer, the browser isn't
allowed to look inside folders, so the game finds the videos by trying 1, 2, 3 and so on
until a number is missing. If you run the game from a local web server, any name works.

Any video with any name can also be added from the app itself: TikTok, then
"Load videos...", then pick them.

Tall videos (like phone videos) fill the phone. For wide videos, press the rotate button
(or R) and the phone turns sideways to fill most of the screen.

A note on GitHub: files over 100 MB can't be uploaded to GitHub, so very big videos stay
on your computer only.
