PHONE WALLPAPERS
================

Put PNG pictures in this folder and they become wallpapers for Miku's phone in Tokyo
(press P in the city, open Settings, and tap one).

Name them with numbers, in order, with no gaps:

    1.png
    2.png
    3.png
    ...

(wallpaper1.png, wallpaper2.png, ... also works.)

Why numbers? When you open index.html straight from your computer, the browser isn't
allowed to look inside folders, so the game finds the pictures by trying 1.png, 2.png,
3.png and so on until one is missing. If you run the game from a local web server, any
name works.

Any picture with any name can also be loaded from the phone itself: Settings, then
"Load pictures...", then pick them from this folder. The one you choose is remembered.

Tall pictures (portrait, like 1080 x 1920) look best.
